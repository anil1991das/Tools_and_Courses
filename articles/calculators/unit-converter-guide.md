# How to Convert Units of Length, Weight, and Temperature

Measurements are used in cooking, construction, travel, science, fitness, shipping, and everyday life. Different countries and industries use different units, so converting a value into another unit is a common task.

A unit converter applies the relationship between two units to calculate an equivalent value. The [iamnoobs Unit Converter](https://iamnoobs.com/unit-converter) supports common categories such as length, weight, and temperature.

## What is unit conversion?

Unit conversion changes the unit used to express a quantity without changing the physical quantity itself.

For example, a distance of one kilometre is the same distance as one thousand metres:

~~~text
1 kilometre = 1,000 metres
~~~

The number changes because the unit changes. The physical distance remains the same.

## Understand conversion factors

A conversion factor is a ratio that relates two units.

For example:

~~~text
1 kilometre = 1,000 metres
1 metre = 100 centimetres
1 kilogram = 1,000 grams
~~~

To convert kilometres to metres, multiply by 1,000. To convert metres to kilometres, divide by 1,000.

### Example: convert 2.5 kilometres to metres

~~~text
2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 metres
~~~

### Example: convert 750 grams to kilograms

~~~text
750 ÷ 1,000 = 0.75 kilograms
~~~

## Common length conversions

| From | To | Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 kilometre | metres | 1,000 m |
| 1 metre | centimetres | 100 cm |
| 1 centimetre | millimetres | 10 mm |
| 1 inch | centimetres | 2.54 cm |
| 1 foot | inches | 12 in |
| 1 mile | kilometres | Approximately 1.609 km |

Use exact conversion factors when precision matters. Values such as miles to kilometres may be rounded for everyday use.

## Common mass conversions

In everyday language, people often say "weight" when referring to mass. In physics, mass and weight are different quantities: mass is measured in kilograms, while weight is a force measured in newtons.

Common mass conversions include:

| From | To | Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 kilogram | grams | 1,000 g |
| 1 gram | milligrams | 1,000 mg |
| 1 metric tonne | kilograms | 1,000 kg |
| 1 pound | kilograms | Approximately 0.453592 kg |
| 1 ounce | grams | Approximately 28.3495 g |

For food, parcels, and ordinary shopping, the common conversion factors are usually sufficient. For laboratory or engineering use, retain enough significant digits for the required accuracy.

## Temperature conversion is different

Length and mass conversions commonly use multiplication or division by a fixed factor. Temperature scales also use an offset, so converting Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin requires a formula.

### Celsius to Fahrenheit

~~~text
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
~~~

Example:

~~~text
25°C = (25 × 9/5) + 32
     = 77°F
~~~

### Fahrenheit to Celsius

~~~text
°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
~~~

### Celsius to Kelvin

~~~text
K = °C + 273.15
~~~

Kelvin is an absolute temperature scale. It is written as K, not °K.

## Significant figures and rounding

A calculator may show many decimal places, but that does not mean the original measurement was known with that precision.

For example, if a distance is measured as approximately 2 metres, converting it to millimetres gives 2,000 millimetres. The extra digits do not make the original measurement more precise.

Follow the significant-figure rules or rounding requirements for the task.

## Common unit conversion mistakes

### 1. Multiplying when you should divide

When converting from a larger unit to a smaller unit, the numerical value usually increases. When converting from a smaller unit to a larger unit, it usually decreases. Check the conversion factor before calculating.

### 2. Confusing mass and weight

Kilograms measure mass; newtons measure force. Everyday contexts often use "weight" informally, but technical work should use the correct quantity.

### 3. Applying a simple ratio to temperature

Temperature conversion includes an offset. Do not convert Celsius to Fahrenheit by multiplying by a factor alone.

### 4. Ignoring unit symbols

A number without a unit can be misleading. Always write the result with its target unit.

### 5. Rounding too early

Keep adequate precision during intermediate calculations and round only when presenting the final result.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a kilogram the same as a litre?

No. A kilogram is a unit of mass and a litre is a unit of volume. Converting between them requires knowing the substance's density.

### Is 1 mile exactly 1.6 kilometres?

The international mile is exactly 1.609344 kilometres. The value 1.6 km is a rounded approximation.

### Why does temperature conversion have an extra number?

The Celsius and Fahrenheit scales have different zero points. Their relationship therefore requires both a scale factor and an offset.
