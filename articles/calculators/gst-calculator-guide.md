# How to Calculate GST on a Price in India

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax applied to supplies of goods and services in India under the applicable GST framework. When preparing an invoice, estimating a selling price, or checking a bill, it is useful to know how the tax amount is calculated and whether the entered price already includes GST.

The [iamnoobs GST Calculator](https://iamnoobs.com/gst-calculator) helps calculate GST amounts using the rate and values entered by the user. This guide explains the arithmetic and the checks that matter.

## What is GST?

GST is a destination-based indirect tax that applies to taxable supplies under India's GST laws. The applicable rate depends on the classification of the goods or services and the relevant legal notifications.

For an intra-State supply, GST is generally represented as Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For an inter-State supply, Integrated GST (IGST) generally applies.

The calculator can perform arithmetic, but it cannot determine the legal classification or tax treatment of a transaction by itself.

## GST on a price before tax

When the entered price does not include GST, calculate the tax as:

~~~text
GST amount = Taxable value × GST rate / 100

Total price = Taxable value + GST amount
~~~

Example using an illustrative 18% rate and a taxable value of ₹1,000:

~~~text
GST amount = 1,000 × 18 / 100
           = ₹180

Total = 1,000 + 180
      = ₹1,180
~~~

The rate in this example is for demonstrating the formula only. Always verify the rate that applies to the actual product or service.

## GST included in a price

Sometimes a quoted price already includes GST. In that case, the tax portion must be extracted from the inclusive amount.

The formula is:

~~~text
GST amount = Inclusive price × GST rate / (100 + GST rate)

Taxable value = Inclusive price - GST amount
~~~

Example using an illustrative inclusive amount of ₹1,180 at 18%:

~~~text
GST amount = 1,180 × 18 / 118
           = ₹180

Taxable value = 1,180 - 180
              = ₹1,000
~~~

This is different from simply taking 18% of ₹1,180, because the tax is already included in the quoted amount.

## CGST, SGST, and IGST

For an intra-State taxable supply where CGST and SGST apply, the applicable GST rate is generally split equally between the two components.

For example, if an illustrative total GST rate is 18%, the split would be:

~~~text
CGST = 9%
SGST = 9%
~~~

For an inter-State supply, IGST generally applies at the combined rate.

The correct tax treatment depends on the place of supply, supplier location, recipient details, and applicable GST rules. Do not choose a tax component only from the customer's city name without checking the actual transaction conditions.

## Common GST calculation mistakes

### 1. Applying tax twice

If a price already includes GST, do not add the same tax again. Use the inclusive-price formula.

### 2. Selecting an incorrect GST rate

Rates depend on classification and current law. Do not assume every product or service uses the same rate.

### 3. Confusing intra-State and inter-State supplies

CGST/SGST and IGST are applied under different supply conditions. Verify the place-of-supply treatment.

### 4. Ignoring rounding

Invoice systems may apply rounding at line-item or invoice-total level. Follow the relevant accounting and tax requirements.

### 5. Treating a calculation as tax advice

A mathematical result does not confirm that a supply is taxable at the chosen rate or that an exemption applies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I calculate GST quickly?

For a GST-exclusive value, multiply the taxable value by the rate and divide by 100. Add the tax amount to the taxable value to get the total.

### How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Multiply the inclusive price by the GST rate and divide by 100 plus the GST rate. Subtract the resulting tax amount to get the taxable value.

### Does the GST Calculator tell me the correct tax rate?

No. You must determine the applicable rate from the current GST classification and rules. The calculator applies the rate you enter or select.
